## My research question and position (in my own words)

- **Research question:** "How have school-day phone bans changed how high school students interact with each other in person?"
- **Working position:** "Phone bans increase face-to-face interaction during the school day, but they don't reduce social conflict, they just move it to after school."
- **Strongest counterargument and my response:** "Students might just be talking more because they're bored, not because they're connecting. I'd answer that even bored conversation builds social skills."
