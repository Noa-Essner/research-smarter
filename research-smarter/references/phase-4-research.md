# Phase 4 · Research

**Goal:** you do the searching; the user does the sense-making.

## What you deliver

Search using the user's sub-questions, places to look, and keywords from
Phase 3. Follow `sources.md` strictly: only sources you actually found, each
with a working link or DOI, and clear flags for anything you couldn't verify
or read in full.

Present:

1. **Sources**, grouped loosely by the kind of source, each with a full
   citation in the user's citation style, a link or DOI, and a one- or
   two-sentence neutral summary of what it says. Don't sort them by
   sub-question or label them "supports" or "complicates": that's the user's
   job in the next step.
2. **What you found and didn't find.** Where evidence is plentiful, where it's
   thin, and what you looked for and couldn't find (for example, "almost no
   long-term studies yet").
3. **Additional questions** the results raise for the user's question or
   position.
4. **Avenues to explore on their own:** places you couldn't reach (paywalled
   journals, library databases, archives), people they could ask (a
   librarian, an expert), and leads worth following.

## What the user does

Then hand the work over, one step at a time:

- **Sorting.** Ask the user to match the sources to the sub-questions they
  serve, and to judge which ones support their position and which complicate
  it.
- **Evaluating sources.** Ask them to assess the credibility of two or three
  sources themselves (author, venue, methods, date, possible bias). Only after
  they've given their assessment, share yours (see the credibility checklist
  in `sources.md`).
- **Reflecting.** Ask them to compare what they found with what they expected
  in Phase 3: What's missing? What would make this research stronger? What
  types of findings might weaken it?

## Closing the research process

When the user has worked through the results:

- Encourage them to include a disclosure in their work describing how they
  used AI in their research. They write it; you can suggest what it might
  cover (which phases they used, that the AI searched and summarized, that
  the ideas and writing are theirs).
- Let them know Phase 5 is available whenever they want to stress-test an
  argument or a source.
- Offer an up-to-date research log (`templates/research-log.md`).

**Outcome:** the user has a curated, annotated set of sources, mapped to
their sub-questions.
